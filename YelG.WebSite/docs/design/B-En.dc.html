A profitable business can still run out of cash.

Profit and cash are not the same thing. That is why finance should not stop at reporting profitability. It should answer:

  • Where is the cash?
  • Why is it tied up?
  • When will it come back?
  • What should management do now?

Can sales growth turn into a cash crisis?

Sustainable growth is not measured by sales and profit alone, but by the ability to fund it without putting financial stability at risk.

  • Estimating what growth will need
  • Cash flow forecasting
  • Assessing financing gaps and options
  • Well-planned expansion decisions

FAQ

Common questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Send us your question and a specialist will reply.

Which companies do you work with?+

SMEs and large-sized companies. Our client portfolio spans 10+ industries in Egypt — including industrial, construction, metals, transportation, tourism and food — as well as banks and educational institutions.

Can we outsource our whole finance function?+

Yes. We provide comprehensive accounting and finance outsourcing — from bookkeeping, reconciliations and payroll to financial reporting, treasury, budgeting and tax — or we can support selected areas only.

How do you protect our confidential information?+

Confidentiality is a core value. Our work structure includes a non-disclosure agreement, and we apply strict confidentiality protocols and security measures to all client financial information.

Do you help select and implement ERP systems?+

Yes — from needs assessment and vendor evaluation to project management, data migration, system integration and chart-of-accounts design. For small companies, we also offer a cost-effective SaaS option.

Do you offer training for our team?+

Yes. We deliver programs in finance, banking, accounting, economics and international finance — for example Finance for Non-Financials, Financial Statements Analysis, and Budgeting & Cost Control.

How does an engagement start?+

With a conversation and a needs assessment. We then agree the scope and confidentiality terms before any work begins. Book a consultation to get started.
